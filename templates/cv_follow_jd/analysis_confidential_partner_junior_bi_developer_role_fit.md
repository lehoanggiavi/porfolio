## JD core signal

This JD is primarily a **BI / Power BI Developer** role, not a pure Data Engineer, Data Scientist or AI Engineer role.

The strongest requirements are:

1. Power BI dashboard/report development for business users.
2. Power BI Desktop, Power BI Service and Power Query.
3. DAX measures, calculated columns, CALCULATE and Time Intelligence.
4. Data modeling for BI: Star schema, Snowflake schema, Fact/Dimension tables and relationships.
5. SQL for validation and reconciliation: SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY, subqueries and window functions.
6. Data QA/QC against source systems.
7. Business requirement gathering and translating needs into reporting solutions.
8. Governance: Row-Level Security, Data Dictionary and Business Glossary.
9. Microsoft ecosystem exposure: Azure SQL, Azure Synapse, Microsoft Fabric / OneLake as a plus.
