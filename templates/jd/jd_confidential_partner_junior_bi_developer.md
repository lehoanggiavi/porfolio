## Responsibilities

### Report design and development

- Receive business requirements from departments such as Sales, Marketing, Finance and Academic Services.
- Build visual, highly interactive dashboards/reports on Power BI Desktop / Power BI Service or Microsoft Fabric Power BI experience.
- Standardize visuals and layouts to ensure optimal UX/UI and make reports easy to understand for business users.

### Data modeling and DAX

- Design standard data models such as Star schema / Snowflake schema optimized for reporting.
- Write basic-to-advanced DAX formulas for business KPIs, conversion rates, cohort analysis, YoY, MoM, etc.

### Data preparation and transformation

- Use Power Query (M code) or Dataflows / Fabric Data Factory to clean, transform and connect data from sources such as Lakehouse, SQL Database, SharePoint and APIs.

### Security and governance

- Configure Row-Level Security (RLS) to protect data access by department/role.
- Participate in building a Data Dictionary / Business Glossary with accurate definitions of business terms and KPIs.

### Support and training / Self-service BI

- Support business users in using self-service BI reports.
- Perform data QA/QC to ensure report accuracy against original data sources.

## Requirements

### Education and experience

- Bachelor degree in Information Technology, MIS, Data Science, Statistics, Finance or related fields.
- 1–2 years of practical experience as Power BI Developer / BI Analyst.

### Technical skills

- Proficient in Power BI Desktop, Power BI Service and Power Query.
- Good DAX skills, including calculated measures, calculated columns, CALCULATE and Time Intelligence.
- Strong data modeling knowledge: Star Schema, Fact Table, Dimension Table, 1-N and N-N relationships.
- SQL from basic to intermediate: SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY, subquery and window functions for checking and reconciling data.
- Cloud understanding or orientation toward Microsoft ecosystem such as Azure SQL, Azure Synapse, Microsoft Fabric / OneLake is a strong plus.

### Soft skills

- Business acumen: listen to business users and translate business problems into technical reporting solutions.
- Proactive and careful: ensure accurate metrics and take responsibility for the product.
- Training and communication: communicate well, explain numbers and guide end users.
